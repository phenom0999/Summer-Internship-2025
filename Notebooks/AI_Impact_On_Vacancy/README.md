# AI IMPACT IN ICT JOB POSTINGS

## Observation
![ICT_Professionals](../Vacancy_Trends/outputs/images/ICT%20Professionals.png)

## Questions
Is GenAI the sole cause of the ICT job posting decline since November 2022?

## Key Finding
**No.** GenAI is not the sole cause.

## Evidence

### The Data
Top 5 declining job categories (Jan 2019 → Nov 2025):
1. **Food Preparation Assistants**: -66.9%
2. **Hospitality Workers**: -66.2%
3. **ICT Professionals**: -51.6%
4. **Sales Support Workers**: -42.6%
5. **Protective Service Workers**: -41.9%

### Why This Matters
- Food prep and hospitality **cannot be automated by GenAI** (physical, in-person work)
- Yet they're declining **faster** than ICT roles
- If GenAI were the sole cause, non-automatable sectors should remain stable

## Actual Cause
**Macroeconomic factors** affecting all sectors:
- Interest rate hikes (RBA, mid-2022 onwards)
- Cost-of-living crisis reducing consumer spending
- Post-pandemic labor market correction
- Tech sector "right-sizing" after 2020-21 over-hiring

## Methodology
To verify this conclusion:
1. Normalize all sectors to Oct 2022 baseline (= 100)
2. Plot ICT vs. control groups (hospitality, teaching, nursing)
3. Compare decline timing with:
   - GenAI releases (Nov 2022, Mar 2023)
   - RBA interest rate hikes
4. Check if decline slopes are parallel (= macro) or diverging (= AI-specific)

## Conclusion
The universal decline across both automatable (ICT) and non-automatable (hospitality) sectors points to **broad economic contraction**, not technological displacement.
